[[vault]](https://papereaterstudios.github.io/attention-habitat/)
<p>
this Quartz site was built from my personal Obsidian vault, which I created as a way to map the connections between my consumed pieces of media and their subsequent datapoints.

i chose to collect and organize this data for the first physical iteration of my personal ethnography catalogue, [*Attention Habitat*](https://papereaterstudios.cargo.site).
<br></br>
from here you can:

* admire that graph, zoom in or expand, select a random node, follow the trail to see what's connected

* use the organized folders on the right to navigate the data

* search for your favorite movie, book, or show to see if we have similar tastes

* search for a certain genre or use a random keyword and see what pops up
	
* leave a comment, opinion, recommendation
</p>
